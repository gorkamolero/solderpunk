## Video tutorials

### Moog Minitaur Video Demo — Daniel Fisher
- **URL:** https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=XZmeCnf_VyA
- **Creator/Channel:** Sweetwater (presenter: Daniel Fisher, Moog specialist)
- **Type:** Video
- **Covers:** The definitive feature walkthrough of the Minitaur — Taurus-3-derived 2-VCO architecture, the ladder filter, envelopes, CV/MIDI/USB control, external audio input. The reference "first look" demo; also mirrored on Sweetwater's InSync page (https://www.sweetwater.com/insync/moog-minitaur-video-demo/).

## Written / Courses

### Working With Moog Minitaur (ADSR course)
- **URL:** https://www.adsrsounds.com/product/courses/working-with-moog-minitaur/
- **Creator:** ADSR Sounds (instructor: Jonny Strinati)
- **Type:** Course (video, paid)
- **Length:** ~1 hour video course
- **Covers:** All Minitaur features and functions, setup with Logic Pro, walkthroughs of both the hardware and the Minitaur Editor software, and techniques for combining the Minitaur with other hardware for live performance and production.
- **Verified:** yes (WebSearch + ADSR product page; instructor and ~1hr length corroborated across two independent searches)

### Moog Minitaur with Logic Pro (ADSR tutorial)
- **URL:** https://www.adsrsounds.com/moog-minitaur-tutorials/moog-minitaur-with-logic-pro/
- **Creator:** ADSR Sounds
- **Type:** Tutorial (part of ADSR's Minitaur tutorial series; index at https://www.adsrsounds.com/category/moog-minitaur-tutorials/)
- **Covers:** Integrating the Minitaur with Logic Pro — DAW routing/control workflow. Part of ADSR's dedicated Minitaur tutorial category.
- **Verified:** yes (page exists; appears in ADSR's Minitaur tutorials category index — note: full page body is JS-rendered, so depth was confirmed via the course/category listings rather than the article text)

### Sound On Sound — Moog Minitaur (review / deep dive)
- **URL:** https://www.soundonsound.com/reviews/moog-minitaur
- **Creator:** Sound On Sound (Gordon Reid)
- **Type:** Blog/written (definitive review)
- **Covers:** The most thorough written treatment of the Minitaur's "under the hood" parameters — counts ~20 hidden voicing params (Note Sync, filter KB tracking, glide types, velocity sensitivity, etc.) and how to reach them via CC/Editor. Essential reading for the hidden-parameter layer. (Already the primary citation in the skill's knowledge base.)
- **Verified:** yes (WebFetch confirmed it is the SOS Minitaur review with detailed hidden-parameter coverage)

### Synthesizing Bass For 3 Different Genres With Moog Minitaur (Attack Magazine)
- **URL:** https://www.attackmagazine.com/technique/synth-secrets/synthesizing-bass-for-3-different-genres-with-moog-minitaur/
- **Creator:** Attack Magazine (author: Adam Douglas)
- **Type:** Blog/written tutorial (step-by-step)
- **Covers:** Step-by-step patch recipes for three genres — Techno (rolling sub under the kick), House (sharp buzzy dual-saw), and Hardcore Rave (reverse-envelope "dread bass"). Concrete knob-position instructions; the source behind several recipes in the skill.
- **Verified:** yes (WebFetch confirmed title, author "Adam Douglas", and the 3-genre tutorial content)

### Moog's Minitaur bass synth just got a lot more interesting (CDM)
- **URL:** https://cdm.link/2017/08/moogs-minitaur-bass-synth-just-got-lot-interesting/
- **Creator:** CDM / Create Digital Music (author: Peter Kirn)
- **Type:** Blog/written (firmware feature deep-dive)
- **Covers:** The v2.2 firmware update — new LFO waveforms (square/saw/ramp/S&H) + Filter EG as mod source, hard sync, 128 presets, the free Editor/Librarian, and CV-input assignment / MIDI-to-CV use. Best single article for understanding what v2.2 unlocked.
- **Verified:** yes (WebFetch confirmed title, author "Peter Kirn", v2.2 firmware focus)

### How do I use my Moog Minitaur? (Brossman Gizmos)
- **URL:** https://brossmangizmos.wordpress.com/2017/08/19/how-do-i-use-my-moog-minitaur/
- **Creator:** Martin Brossman — "Technology, Gadgets and Gizmos" blog
- **Type:** Blog/written (resource hub)
- **Covers:** A beginner-oriented hub post: stresses the Minitaur is a sound module needing an external controller/sequencer, then links a 3-part Ableton Live integration series, v2.2 firmware notes, the official manual, pairing with other Moog synths, and community resources (forums/Facebook groups). Good orientation + onward-links page.
- **Verified:** yes (WebFetch confirmed title, author Martin Brossman, and resource-hub content)
