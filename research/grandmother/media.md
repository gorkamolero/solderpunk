# Moog Grandmother — Video & Media Learning Resources

Curated, verified resources for learning the Moog Grandmother semi-modular synth.
Every entry was confirmed to exist and to be about the Grandmother via WebSearch + WebFetch
(June 2026). Where a YouTube channel name could not be read off the page directly (YouTube
serves a consent wall / footer-only HTML to the fetcher), the source that confirms attribution
is named explicitly in the `Verified:` line.

## Video tutorials

### Unlocking Grandmother's Secrets: Review, ideas and tips (Loopop)
- **URL:** https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ciVigeCZiXA
- **Creator/Channel:** loopop (Ziv Eliraz)
- **Type:** Video
- **Covers:** The definitive single-video deep dive. Spring reverb, mixer, patch layout, UI, hard
  sync, modulation, the hidden 3rd oscillator (LFO), paraphonic playing, glide modes, low-pass
  filter, envelopes, arpeggiator & sequencer, feedback/utility tricks, patch ideas, pros & cons.
  Reveals that GM is really 3+ oscillators (4 with the self-resonating filter) and 2–3 envelopes.
- **Length/count:** Single long-form video (~30+ min), published 2018-07-30
- **Verified:** yes (WebSearch confirmed URL + title + creator + publish date; companion blog post
  at loopopmusic.com lists the same timestamped topic outline; Synthtopia "Tips & Tricks" post
  also attributes the video to loopop)
