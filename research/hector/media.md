# Poly Effects Hector & Beebo — Media & Learning Resources

Hector (30 HP Eurorack, touchscreen) and Beebo (pedal) run **identical firmware** ("Beebo OS") and swap presets freely, so virtually all Beebo tutorials, demos, and looper content apply directly to Hector. Resources below are tagged accordingly. Every entry was confirmed via WebSearch + WebFetch to exist and to be about Hector/Beebo; unconfirmable leads were discarded.

> Note on verification depth: YouTube watch/playlist pages serve a GDPR consent wall to this tool, so per-video metadata (exact title, channel, duration) was confirmed through search-result snippets and aggregator pages (Loopop's own site, MatrixSynth) rather than the YouTube page body itself. Where a duration/count is stated it came from a source that exposed it; otherwise it is omitted rather than guessed.
