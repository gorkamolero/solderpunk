# DSI / Sequential Tetra — Video & Media Learning Resources

Curated, verified learning resources for the **Dave Smith Instruments / Sequential Tetra** (4-voice analog poly).
The Tetra shares its core voice architecture (2 DCOs, Curtis low-pass filter, 3 DADSR envelopes, 4 LFOs, mod
matrix, sub-osc, feedback loop, 16x4 gated sequencer) with the **Mopho** and **Prophet '08**, so directly-applicable
content from those instruments is included and **labelled** as such.

Every entry below was confirmed via WebSearch + WebFetch (June 2026). Note: YouTube pages often return only
footer markup to automated fetchers, so for video titles/channels the verification method is recorded honestly
(search-result confirmation vs. full page fetch). Unconfirmable resources were discarded.

Discarded as unverifiable / not actually learning content:
- Sonic State "Sonic LAB: DSI Tetra" review video — the embedded clip is dead Flash ("video has been depreciated"); no working link.
- synth-patches.com / Alba Ecstasy "Science" pack — commercial preset sales pages, no real tutorial content.

## Written / Courses

### Pym's DSI Dev — "NRPNs Part 1: The Basics" (+ Parts 2 & 3)
- **URL:** http://tetradev.blogspot.com/2010/03/nrpns-part-1-basics.html
- **Creator/channel:** "Pym's DSI Dev" blog
- **Type:** Blog (3-part technical series)
- **Covers:** The definitive plain-English explanation of **NRPN** control for DSI synths (Mopho/Tetra). Part 1 = how NRPNs work (CC 99/98 MSB/LSB pair + CC 6/38 value, building a 14-bit value, raw MIDI byte walkthrough). Part 2 = "Filtering NRPNs in Ableton with M4L". Part 3 = "Sending with Ableton and M4L". Essential for anyone automating the Tetra from a DAW or controller, since NRPN is the Tetra's preferred parameter-control method.
- **Length:** 3 posts
- **Verified:** yes (WebFetch confirmed blog name, post title, date, and the existence/titles of Parts 2 and 3).

### Sound on Sound — DSI Tetra review (Gordon Reid, March 2010)
- **URL:** https://www.soundonsound.com/reviews/dave-smith-instruments-tetra
- **Creator/channel:** Sound on Sound (author: Gordon Reid)
- **Type:** Blog / in-depth written review
- **Covers:** Substantial, technically-detailed walkthrough of the Tetra: full voice architecture vs. Mopho/Prophet '08; a 14-permutation table of voicing modes (Program, Stack, Split, Combo, unison variants); the **sub-oscillators and feedback loop**; and the Pro editor (Snapshots, Genetics, Morpher). The mode table alone is a top reference for understanding Combo/Multi behaviour. Honest critiques included (limited arpeggiator, no power switch).
- **Length:** long-form article
- **Verified:** yes (WebFetch confirmed author Gordon Reid, March 2010, and the depth of architecture / combo-mode / feedback / editor coverage).

### Carillon Audio — "Programming more expressive synth sounds: DSI Mopho (or any synth)"  *(Mopho — directly applicable)*
- **URL:** https://carillonaudio.wordpress.com/2016/03/20/programming-more-expressive-synth-sounds-dsi-mopho-or-any-synth/
- **Creator/channel:** Carillon Audio (WordPress blog)
- **Type:** Blog (written sound-design tutorial)
- **Covers:** Five practical **mod-matrix** techniques on the Mopho (same engine as one Tetra voice), each with a routing diagram: velocity → VCA env / attack / sustain; aftertouch → vibrato and filter cutoff; aux envelope → noise volume; envelope → LFO depth (pitch warble). Aimed at getting two "voices" out of one patch through dynamics. Technique transfers 1:1 to the Tetra.
- **Length:** single in-depth post (5 techniques)
- **Verified:** yes (WebFetch confirmed the post, the five techniques, and that it's a practical mod-matrix tutorial on the Mopho).
