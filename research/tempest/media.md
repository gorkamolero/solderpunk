# Tempest — Video & Media Learning Resources

Curated, verified learning resources for the **DSI / Sequential Tempest** analog drum machine
(designed by Dave Smith + Roger Linn). Every entry below was confirmed via WebSearch + WebFetch
to exist and to actually cover the Tempest. Anything that couldn't be confirmed was discarded.

Note on YouTube verification: YouTube watch/playlist pages do not reliably expose channel names
to a text fetcher, so channel/creator attribution was cross-confirmed via the official Sequential
site, MatrixSynth, creator websites, and search-result metadata rather than the video page itself.

## Written tutorials / Courses / Reference

### NoisyLittleBugger — "Tempest Recipes" series
- **Hub URL:** https://noisylittlebugger.net/tempest-recipes/

- **Creator:** NoisyLittleBugger (blog; also mirrored at stimresp.wordpress.com)
- **Type:** Blog (multi-part written tutorial series)
- **Covers:** The deepest *written* sound-design resource for the Tempest. Each article gives spectral
  analysis of the target sound plus exact step-by-step patch instructions. Confirmed installments:
  - First things first — https://noisylittlebugger.net/2012/02/19/tempest-recipes-first-things-first/
  - Hi-Hats, Shakers, Zaps — https://noisylittlebugger.net/2012/03/04/tempest-recipes-hi-hats-shakers-zaps/
  - Snare Drums — https://noisylittlebugger.net/2012/03/31/tempest-recipes-snare-drums/
  - Bells and alien idiophones — https://noisylittlebugger.net/2013/02/06/tempest-recipes-bells-and-alien-idiophones/
  - Donk Bass — https://stimresp.wordpress.com/2013/01/15/tempest-recipes-donk-bass/
  - Sub-bass — https://stimresp.wordpress.com/2013/01/16/tempest-recipes-sub-bass/
- **Verified:** yes (WebFetch confirmed the Snare Drums article is a detailed Tempest patch tutorial; WebSearch confirmed the hub plus the kick/hi-hat/clap/bass/bells/sub-bass installments by title and URL)

### AudioCookbook — "DSI Tempest Parameter-Lock-Like Technique"
- **URL:** https://audiocookbook.org/parameter-lock-simulated-on-the-dsi-tempest/
- **Creator:** John CS Keston (AudioCookbook.org), Jan 2013
- **Type:** Blog (written technique tutorial)
- **Covers:** A clever workaround for the Tempest's lack of Elektron-style parameter locks: copy one
  patch across all 16 pads, vary each slightly, and/or play in parts from an external keyboard while
  tweaking system settings live during recording. Niche but genuinely useful sequencer trick.
- **Verified:** yes (WebFetch confirmed the article title, author, date, and that it details the parameter-lock simulation method on the DSI Tempest)

### Attack Magazine — DSI Tempest (in-depth review)
- **URL:** https://www.attackmagazine.com/reviews/gear-software/dsi-tempest/
- **Creator:** Greg Scarth (Attack Magazine), Nov 2012
- **Type:** Blog (deep written review)
- **Covers:** Substantial analysis of the sound engine and drum-synthesis versatility, compared to the
  TR-808/909, written from a producer's perspective (post-v1.2 firmware). Good for understanding
  *why* you'd program things a certain way.
- **Verified:** yes (WebFetch confirmed title, author Greg Scarth, Nov 23 2012, and the deep-dive content)
